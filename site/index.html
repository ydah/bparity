Keep the evidence.
Leave the legacy.

Record while the old environment still runs. A portable Specification Bundle becomes the reference for everything that comes next.

  1. 1

    Capture the original

    Define the public boundary and run your RSpec or Minitest suite. Combine recorded traces and source evidence into a checked bundle.

    record + synthesize

  2. 2

    Map the replacement

    Use a small Ruby adapter to connect recorded operations to your new API. Classes, method names, and argument shapes can all change.

    .bparity/adapter.rb

  3. 3

    Check what changed

    Verify with just the bundle, adapter, and replacement. Inspect differences and declared waivers in Markdown, JSON, JUnit XML, or HTML.

    bparity verify

Know what passed.
And what was checked.

Start with recorded examples. Add property checks, finite-domain exploration, model comparison, or Z3 proofs when the required inputs are available.

Every formal result reports its scope, assumptions, and exclusions. Inconclusive results stay inconclusive.
